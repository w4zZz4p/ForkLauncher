ForkLauncher is a personal open-source launcher for Android TV, forked from Arc Launcher by Badis Meddouri.

What this fork adds:

- Folder slideshow - Random wallpaper from a folder on the TV, changed on a timer you choose, with no internet needed.
- Wallpaper-only view - Press Home on the home screen to hide everything but the wallpaper.
- Clean home screen - Sections stay below the screen edge until you press Down from the dock.

Everything else comes from Arc Launcher and the projects before it: LTvLauncher by LeanBitLab, and FLauncher by etienn01 and osrosal.
